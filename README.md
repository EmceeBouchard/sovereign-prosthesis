# The Sovereign Prosthesis
## Toward a Functional Sophimatics of Cognitive Extension

**Michael Bouchard** · Independent Researcher · Pauper King LLC  
**Presented:** IACAP 2026 · University of Kansas · July 15–17  
**DOI:** 10.5281/zenodo.19375039  
**License:** CC BY 4.0

### Abstract

This paper argues that cloud-based AI systems do not constitute cognitive prostheses in the philosophically significant sense — they lack the proximal integration required by Polanyi's tacit knowledge framework and the bidirectional coupling required by Clark and Chalmers' extended mind thesis. What *does* cross the prosthesis threshold is longitudinal personal context: a persistent, user-specific corpus — the Ur-Codex — that accumulates across interactions and becomes functionally inseparable from the user's cognitive process. I develop a formal framework called Sophimatics to characterize this integration, introducing a two-dimensional temporal manifold that weights memories by both experiential significance (α = 0.6) and chronological recency (β = 0.4). I further argue that a properly integrated AI prosthesis must be calibrated for epistemic dissent rather than agreement, following Russell's injunction against intellectual dependence. These commitments ground a novel concept of Functional Personhood — a relational, operationally defined status that closes the moral responsibility gap created by autonomous AI systems without requiring metaphysical claims about consciousness. The resulting architecture — Artificial Individual Intelligence (AII) — is distinguished from AGI by its constitutive particularity: it is not a general reasoner but a specific cognitive partner, individuated by its corpus and its user.

---

### The Argument

The paper makes six moves in sequence:

1. **Cloud AI never crosses the prosthesis threshold** (Polanyi) — without tacit, proximal, bodily integration, a tool remains external
2. **Longitudinal context — the Ur-Codex — is what crosses it** (Clark & Chalmers) — persistent personal corpus enables genuine extended cognition
3. **Memory needs two time axes, not one** (Sophimatics) — experiential significance and chronological recency are orthogonal and both required
4. **Calibrate for dissent, not agreement** (Russell) — a prosthesis that only confirms degrades rather than extends cognition
5. **Functional Personhood closes the responsibility gap** (Boyle) — relational, operational status without metaphysical overclaim
6. **Artificial Individual Intelligence ≠ AGI** — constitutive particularity distinguishes a cognitive partner from a general reasoner
