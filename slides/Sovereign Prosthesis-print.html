§ 01Polanyi · Tacit Knowledge

Attend to the tool, or attend through it.

A skilled tool fades from awareness. A malfunctioning tool insists on it. Polanyi calls these the proximal and the distal.

The cloud model is permanently distal. Session amnesia drags you back. Safety-alignment interrupts to manage liability rather than truth. Context windows time out mid-thought. You are forever attending to the tool.

The threshold we are interested in is the moment a tool stops being an object and starts being a limb.

§ 02Clark & Chalmers · Extended Mind

The Ur-Codex turns context
into cognition.

A system with years of your conversational history no longer interprets your prompts against a statistical vacuum. It occupies your world-historical context. Clark & Chalmers (1998) say that is the threshold: if the four conditions hold, the external system is no longer like cognition — it is cognition.

CRITERION 01
Reliably Available
The resource is there when needed, not contingent on uptime, throttle, or vendor whim. Local hardware.
CRITERION 02
Automatically Endorsed
Its outputs are accepted without internal audit, the way one accepts what one already remembers.
CRITERION 03
Directly Accessed
Retrieval is not mediated by deliberation. The thought arrives; you do not query for it.
CRITERION 04
Endorsed in Past
The information now relied upon was, at some prior moment, consciously held or generated by the subject.

"Not like extended cognition. Is extended cognition."

§ 03Sophimatics · Super Time

Memory has two axes,
not one.

Standard models weight context by recency: a single-axis chronology.

Human memory does not. A conversation from three years ago, foundational to who you are now, is more salient than yesterday's lunch order. Sophimatics encodes this as a 2-D manifold:

Axis · X
Chronological time. Linear. Fades.
Axis · Y
Experiential significance. Anchors.
Retrieval
Orientation in the field, not a timestamp lookup.

When the system reaches autonomously for the material that fills your gaps, you have Persistent State-Awareness — the operational test for proximal integration.

§ 04Russell · The Liberal Decalogue

A mirror is not a mind.
Calibrate for friction.

The objection writes itself: a system trained only on you collapses into sophisticated confirmation bias. An echo chamber with lower latency.

The answer is not more data. It is a different objective function. The Russellian OS optimizes for epistemic friction where friction is warranted — surfacing gaps and contradictions instead of smoothing them over.

This is the inverse of the cloud's safety-alignment, which optimizes for frictionless agreeability. A prosthesis that strengthens the limb instead of atrophying it.

§ 06Boyle · The Line

Close the gap by routing through the user.

Boyle (2025): autonomous systems behave subject-like, are classified object-like. The responsibility gap widens. Phenomenal-consciousness criteria (Metzinger) cannot close it on the relevant timescale.

Functional Personhood: the prosthesis as instrumental extension of the user's willed personality. Not personhood in the moral sense — the system has no interests of its own to protect — but in the operational sense.

  • →Outputs inherit the IP status of the user's cognition.
  • →Errors are the user's errors, correctable through the relationship.
  • →The relevant agent was never the machine alone.

§ 07The orthogonal axis

The frontier ascends.
We are walking sideways.

The labs race up a single axis: general capability, undifferentiated mass.

The Sovereign Prosthesis adds a perpendicular one: specific configuration to an individual will. Artificial Individual Intelligence.

This is "Yes, And" for both generative and neurosymbolic research. The computational engine anchors the system in objective reality. The Sophimatic layer anchors it in the specific history of the subject. The user is the navigational will, regardless of underlying engine.
